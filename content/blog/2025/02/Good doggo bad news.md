OK fine, I cracked.

If you install my extension you can now get random pictures of dogs instead of US news on the BBC website. It uses <https://placedog.net/> to provide the dog photos.

Installation instructions are [at the bottom of my last post](https://philwilson.org/blog/2025/02/no-more-us-news-on-the-bbc-homepage/). 

It takes a few seconds to load because of some JavaScript that runs on the BBC page, but it should work OK in most places. Let me know with a webmention or [on mastodon](https://mastodon.me.uk/@pip) if it fails horribly.

Again, is it worth it? Well for me it just replaced a story about Prince Harry and Meghan Markle with a photo of a labrador. You decide.
