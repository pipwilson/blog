I have a [Mecool KM2 Plus Android TV](https://www.mecool.com/products/tv-box-mecool-km2-plus) which I have in my garage so I can watch Netflix or whatever whilst [riding my bike online](https://philwilson.org/blog/2023/02/riding-my-bike-online/). Generally speaking I really like this box.

I also have some [Samsung Galaxy Buds FE earbuds](https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samsung_Galaxy_Buds_series) but if you try and pair them to the Mecool box (via the "Accessories" menu) it doesn't work.

This is because of the xcpe settings. If you disable this then the pairing will work smoothly. You can do this by opening Settings -> Device Preferences -> Accessibility and then selecting XCPE at the bottom of the list.

The real benefit of doing this right now as it hits 30C in the UK is that I can leave my garage door open whilst on my bike and not be blasting the whole neighbourhood with the latest episode of [My Hero Academia](https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Hero_Academia)!
