[Moondial](https://uk.bookshop.org/p/books/moondial-helen-cresswell/1431998) - I actually got this out from the library for my daughter to read over Halloween but she wasn't interested and so I read it, having seen a few episodes of the TV version in the late 80s. This was surprisingly spooky! It was also quite entertaining despite the loose ends and unresolved questions. A good ghost story for the 10/11 year olds.

[We Have Always Lived in the Castle ](https://uk.bookshop.org/p/books/we-have-always-lived-in-the-castle-shirley-jackson/2650223?ean=9780241685044)was good and fun to read, but nothing like what I expected since it was a gothic mystery and I was expecting something a little more mundane-literary-fiction. Most of the characters were suitably insane, orbiting irregularly around a single fixed point
