In November I was still not in the mood for reading and so made my way through [Hail Mary](https://uk.bookshop.org/p/books/project-hail-mary-from-the-bestselling-author-of-the-martian-andy-weir/5047890?ean=9781529157468) AGAIN and [Vera Wong's Unsolicited Advice for Murderers](https://uk.bookshop.org/p/books/vera-wong-s-unsolicited-advice-for-murderers-jesse-sutanto/7011813) which was broadly pleasant, but very silly and parts of it were actually very badly written (like different people's internal thoughts being completely indistinguishable due to lack of personalities), and it cashes in on the current craze for cozy murder mysteries which exploded after Richard Osman's The Thursday Murder Club came out in 2020. An interesting structure and protagonist though!
