I was tired in September, so I read the 7 books of [the Murderbot Diaries]([The Murderbot Diaries - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Murderbot_Diaries)). Again. There's something about a these which are absolute comfort reading for me.

After [enjoying Hardwired earlier in the year]([Books of April 2024 - philwilson.org](https://philwilson.org/blog/2024/05/books-of-april-2024/)) I picked up [Daddy's World]([Daddy's World eBook : Williams, Walter Jon: Amazon.co.uk: Kindle Store](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B00B2152Y0)) (also [on archive.org]([Daddy's World : Walter Jon Williams : Free Download, Borrow, and Streaming : Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/daddys-world/mode/2up))), a short story by Walter Jon Williams. It was great but also a horrifying vision of what's not knowing what's real, and then finding out.

And that was September.
