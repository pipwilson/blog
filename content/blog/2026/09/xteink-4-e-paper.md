After reading all the great reviews I finally bought myself an XTEink 4. This is a small e-paper device with a 4.3" screen (an iPhone 17 has a x" screen) and flashable firmware, meaning it can be an office pass, or a an e-reader, or a TRMNL device (https://help.trmnl.com/en/articles/13563787-connect-your-xteink-x4-to-trmnl)

The one I bought has been discontinued, but I got mine from eBay because it has a USB-C port instead of some proprietary pogo pins which means you need to use a USB adapter to charge it.

I normally read a dozen or so books a year (which means that if I live to the UK average male life expectancy of xx and am still capable of reading then I have 12*xx books left to read before I die) but have fallen off the wagon recently, in particular losing my free time to scrolling inanely instead.

This device is so small that I can stick it in my pocket alongside my phone (the XTEink 3 is small enough to attach to the magnet on the back of an iPhone!) and it gives me a physical reminder that I could be reading a book instead, and the means to do it without getting distracted.

Mine came preinstalled with CrossPoint, the most popular e-reader firmware for this device, and syncs with any KOReader server, which my normal Android eBook app(Readest) on my phone and eInk tablet can also use, meaning I can sync reading progress (although not the books themselves) between all three. 

So far it's been a really good experience, and for £55 definitely worth it.
