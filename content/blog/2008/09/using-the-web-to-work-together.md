Next Wednesday, intrepid Web Servicer Phil Wilson will be giving the third of our [Web Sessions](http://blogs.bath.ac.uk/webservices/category/web-sessions/). Following the last talk about consuming information from the web ("The Other Side of the Web - RSS and Atom Feeds" which [you can watch online here](http://www.bath.ac.uk/marketing/webservices/presentations/websessions/feeds/)), this one will be a brief look at some different ways of communicating and collaborating with other people in your team, the University, and the world.

We'll be in 3W 3.7 on Wednesday the 10th of September at 12:15 so come along and join us!
